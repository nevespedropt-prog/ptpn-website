What gets measured

Four things we track from your first session onward.

01 · Body

Weight and body fat

Weighed and measured consistently, not guessed from how your jeans feel. Two lines on a chart that tell the truth about the last month.

02 · Strength

Every lift you make

Sets, reps and weight are logged session by session, so your personal bests are a record rather than a memory.

03 · Nutrition

What you actually eat

Calorie and macro targets set by me, with a food diary in the app. No guesswork about why progress stalled.

04 · Consistency

Whether you turned up

Attendance, completed sessions and weekly check-ins. Consistency is the variable that decides the result, so we watch it.

05 · Coaching

A plan that changes

The programme is rewritten as the numbers move. What worked in month one is not what gets you through month six.

06 · Review

Evidence, not opinion

Every few weeks we sit down with the data and decide what to change. Coaching is a loop, and you get to see it working.

Process

How coaching works, start to finish.

Step 01

Enquiry

You tell me your goal, your training history and the days that realistically work. I reply with the package that fits and the next available slot.

Step 02

Baseline assessment

Weight, body fat, measurements and a movement screen. This is the set of numbers everything else gets compared against.

Step 03

Programme written

Your plan is built and loaded into the app: the exercises, the sets, the reps, the weights. You can see exactly what you are doing and why.

Step 04

Train, log, review

We train together, you log your sessions, and every few weeks we review the data and adjust the plan. Then the loop repeats.

Questions

Personal training, answered.

How much does personal training cost?

Packages start at £32.50 a session for Express, a twelve-session 30-minute pack. The one-hour packs are Ignition at £54 a session for four sessions, Momentum at £48 for eight, and Signature at £40 for twelve. Duo, the small group option, is £60 a month for a minimum of two people training at least once a week. All prices are per session and paid in person.

Is the first session free?

Yes. I offer a free consultation with no obligation. We talk through your goal, your training history and what has stopped you before, and you leave with a clear idea of what working together would look like. There is no charge and no pressure to book anything.

Do I need experience before I start?

No. Plenty of clients have never trained properly before. The first session is an assessment, and the programme is written around what you can do now.

How many sessions a week do I need?

Most clients train two or three times a week. If you train with me less often, I will give you work to do alone and you will log it in the app so I can see it.

What happens if I miss a session?

Speak to me and we rearrange. A package is a block of sessions rather than a weekly contract, so a missed week does not cost you a session.

Where do we train?

In a private studio in Bush Hill Park, Enfield. [Confirm exact address before publishing.]

Is the app extra?

No. The PTPN app comes with coaching. It holds your programme, your logging and your progress at no extra cost.
