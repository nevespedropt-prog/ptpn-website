Why one-to-one

Most people do not need more motivation. They need a plan and someone watching the numbers.

01

Programmed for you

Not a generic plan off the internet. Your programme is written around your training history, your injuries and the equipment you actually have access to.

02

Technique that holds up

Every rep is watched and corrected. That is what keeps you training in ten years rather than ten weeks.

03

Progress you can see

Weight, body fat, measurements and personal bests, all logged. We adjust the plan on evidence, not guesswork.

What gets measured

Six things we track from your first session onward.

This is the part most coaching leaves out. You cannot manage what you never measure, so we measure everything that matters.

01 · Body

Weight and body fat

Measured consistently, not guessed from how your jeans feel. Two lines on a chart that tell the truth about the last month.

02 · Strength

Every lift you make

Sets, reps and weight logged session by session, so your personal bests are a record rather than a memory.

03 · Nutrition

What you actually eat

Calorie and macro targets set by me, with a food diary and meal plans in the app. No guessing why progress stalled.

04 · Consistency

Whether you turned up

Attendance, completed sessions and weekly check-ins. Consistency decides the result, so we watch it.

05 · Coaching

A plan that changes

The programme is rewritten as the numbers move. What worked in month one is not what gets you through month six.

06 · Review

Evidence, not opinion

Every few weeks we sit down with the data and decide what to change. Coaching is a loop, and you get to watch it work.

Questions

Personal training, answered.

How much does personal training cost?

Packages start at £32.50 a session for Express, a twelve-session 30-minute pack. The one-hour packs are Ignition at £54 a session for four sessions, Momentum at £48 for eight, and Signature at £40 for twelve. Duo, the small group option, is £60 a month for a minimum of two people training at least once a week. All prices are per session and paid in person.

Is the first session free?

Yes. I offer a free consultation with no obligation. We talk through your goal, your training history and what has stopped you before, and you leave with a clear idea of what working together would look like. There is no charge and no pressure to book anything.

Do I need experience before I start?

No. Plenty of clients have never trained properly before. The first session is an assessment, and the programme is written around what you can do now, not what you think you should be able to do.

How many sessions a week do I need?

Most clients train two or three times a week. If you train with me less often than that, I will give you work to do on your own and you will log it in the app so I can see it.

What happens if I miss a session?

Speak to me and we rearrange. A package is a block of sessions rather than a weekly contract, so a missed week does not cost you a session.

Where do we train?

In a private studio in Bush Hill Park, Enfield. [Confirm exact address before publishing.]

Is the app extra?

No. The PTPN app comes with coaching. It holds your programme, your logging and your progress, and it costs you nothing on top. You can sign in on your phone or on the website.

Do I have to use the app?

No, but it is how the coaching actually works between sessions. If you would rather not, tell me and we will find a way that suits you.
